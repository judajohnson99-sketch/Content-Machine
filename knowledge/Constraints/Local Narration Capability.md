# Local Narration Capability

**Piper TTS is installed locally: offline, free, roughly 0.33x realtime, and
byte-deterministic with noise scales zeroed.**

Voice `en_US-libritts_r-medium` is **CC BY 4.0** — commercial use permitted,
**attribution required**. The attribution obligation is a
[[Rights and Licensing]] matter and must ride in the audio manifest, not in
someone's memory.

## Consequence

This unblocked every `tts_required` concept at zero marginal cost, which is what
made [[Bedtime Stories]] producible at all and put the narrated explainer in
[[Non-Sleep Alternatives]] within reach.

Determinism matters beyond tidiness: it means a narrated render is reproducible
from its plan, so the [[Publication Gate]]'s digest over its inputs stays
meaningful for narrated projects too.

**Its limit is untested.** The quality bar for a sleeping listener is low and
comfortably met — see the sleepy-history renders in [[Experiment Log]]. The bar
for an attentive listener is much higher and remains an open item in
[[Open Research Questions]], alongside the pause and pacing control that guided
meditation would need.

Recorded operationally in `experiments/concepts.json` under
`production_constraints.narration_available`.
