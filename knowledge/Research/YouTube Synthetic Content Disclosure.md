# YouTube Synthetic Content Disclosure

**Scope is VERIFIED from the official policy. The conclusion drawn for TTS
below is INFERENCE from that scope, and is labelled as such.**

Disclosure of "altered or synthetic content" is required when AI is used to
make a real person appear to say or do something they did not, to alter footage
of a real event or place, or to generate a realistic scene that did not occur.

Explicitly exempt: unrealistic or animated content, minor aesthetic edits, and
production assistance such as AI-written scripts.

## Implication

**INFERENCE:** generic TTS narration and abstract procedural plates fall
outside the trigger, which is realistic depiction of real people, events or
places. That keeps [[Local Narration Capability]] and the procedural visuals
described in [[Provenance and Production-Grade Claims]] clear of it.

**Consequence for sleepy history in [[Bedtime Stories]]:** satisfying its "dim
archival-style stills" requirement with AI-generated archival-looking
photographs would generate realistic imagery of a real place and era that never
existed as depicted. That does plausibly trigger disclosure, and it undermines
a video whose value proposition is being factual. Genuine public-domain
archival photography avoids both problems and satisfies the visual requirement
more honestly. **Prefer public-domain sourcing over generation for this
concept.**
