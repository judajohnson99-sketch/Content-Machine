# Open Research Questions

Assumptions that must be verified before real production time is spent against
them. Every one is drawn from `assumptions_requiring_research` in
`experiments/concepts.json`, which stays the authoritative list.

## Ranked by what they would change

1. **RPM differential between explainer and ambient categories.** Named in the
   concept file as the single most valuable unknown. A large gap redirects the
   entire portfolio toward [[Non-Sleep Alternatives]].
2. **Made-for-Kids realised ad revenue.** Decisive for the whole of
   [[Kids Sleep]]; nothing in that niche is worth producing until it is known.
3. **Whether extreme-length uploads are favoured or suppressed by
   recommendation.** Bears directly on the 3-8h formats in [[Adult Sleep]] and
   [[Focus Ambience]].
4. **Whether generated music can be cleanly licensed for monetized commercial
   use.** Blocks the melodic half of [[Kids Sleep]] and the lo-fi concept in
   [[Focus Ambience]]. A [[Rights and Licensing]] question.
5. **Whether awake-audience ad engagement genuinely differs.** The premise
   [[Focus Ambience]] rests on; needs real channel data, not desk research.
6. **Whether still-based slow TV is accepted or motion footage is required.**
   Decides the scenery concept in [[Non-Sleep Alternatives]].

## Resolved

- Monetization exposure of static ambient output → [[YouTube Inauthentic Content Policy]].
- Disclosure obligations for synthetic narration and imagery → [[YouTube Synthetic Content Disclosure]].
- TTS availability and cost for long-form narration → [[Local Narration Capability]].
- Audio loop seams for generated sources → resolved by synthesising at the exact
  target duration, so no loop exists to seam.

## Still open, sourcing rather than research

Licence terms for rain, wave and cafe recordings, and per-asset licence status
for space imagery. These are procurement questions under
[[Rights and Licensing]], not open unknowns.
