# YouTube Inauthentic Content Policy

**Quoted from official YouTube channel monetization policy. The "repetitious
content" policy was renamed "inauthentic content" on 2025-07-15.**

Explicitly not allowed to monetize:

- "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the
  impression of mass production"
- "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no
  narrative"

The required standard: "the substance of each video should be materially varied
and deliver creative, educational, or other value."

## Implication

This resolved the open assumption on the brown-noise concept in
[[Adult Sleep]]. A single static plate plus a generated noise bed is the least
materially varied output this pipeline can make and carries minimal narrative
or educational value, so it sits closest to the named prohibition.

This does **not** mean ambient channels cannot monetize — many demonstrably do.
It means monetization is the specific risk to measure first, exactly as that
concept's kill evidence already states.

Narrated concepts whose script differs materially per video are substantially
better positioned, which is the strategic argument for [[Bedtime Stories]] and
the narrated explainer in [[Non-Sleep Alternatives]]. It also favours the
structured-session concept in [[Focus Ambience]] over static ones.
