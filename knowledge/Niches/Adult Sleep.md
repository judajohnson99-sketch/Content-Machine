# Adult Sleep

Sound-masking content for adults who sleep with audio: light-sensitive
sleepers, shift workers, roughly 25-55. Five concepts sit in this niche
(`niche: adult_sleep` in `experiments/concepts.json`): brown noise on a dark
screen, rain on glass, storm-and-cabin-fire, night ocean waves, and space
drift.

**The bet.** Very long sessions (3-8h) accumulate watch time, and the audience
returns nightly. Every part of that is an assumption; none of it is measured.

**Why it moved first.** Brown noise is the only concept in the portfolio with
no external dependency at all — the bed is synthesised by ffmpeg and the plate
is procedural, so production complexity and generation dependence are both at
the floor. It is the cheapest way to learn something real, which is why
[[Experiment Log]] starts here.

**What it is most exposed to.** Commoditisation — thousands of near-identical
uploads already exist — and, more sharply, the monetization risk described in
[[YouTube Inauthentic Content Policy]]. A single static plate over a generated
noise bed is the least materially varied output this pipeline can produce, so
it sits closest to the named prohibition. Ambient channels demonstrably do
monetize; the point is that monetization is the first thing to measure here,
not an assumption to build on.

The other four concepts are gated on sourcing, not capability: rain, wave and
storm beds all need a provably licence-clean source ([[Rights and Licensing]]),
and the space concept needs per-asset licence status for its imagery.

Dark visuals in this niche are bounded from below by
[[Dark Visual Luminance Floor]], and their length is bounded from above by
[[Render Throughput]].
