# Bedtime Stories

Narrated long-form: public-domain fairy tales, original gentle stories, and
deliberately monotone "sleepy history" — three concepts under
`niche: bedtime_stories`, 25-60 minutes.

**This niche is the portfolio's hedge against
[[YouTube Inauthentic Content Policy]].** The policy's standard is that the
substance of each video be materially varied and carry creative or educational
value. A narrated video whose script genuinely differs per upload is far better
positioned than a static ambient plate, so the same production capability
placed behind narration is worth materially more.

**It became producible when narration did.** [[Local Narration Capability]]
unblocked every `tts_required` concept here at zero marginal cost, and
[[Experiment Log]] records the first two sleepy-history renders.

**Its sharpest constraint is visual, not audio.** Sleepy history calls for dim
archival-style stills. Satisfying that with AI-generated archival-looking
photographs would produce realistic imagery of a real place and era that never
existed as depicted — which plausibly trips
[[YouTube Synthetic Content Disclosure]] and undermines a video whose entire
value proposition is being factual. Genuine public-domain archival photography
avoids both problems. Prefer sourcing over generation here.

Remaining open items: public-domain status of the specific *translation* used
(not merely the original tale), and the cost of a fact-verification workflow
per script.
