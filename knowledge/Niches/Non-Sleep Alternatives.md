# Non-Sleep Alternatives

Four concepts held deliberately outside the ambient thesis
(`niche: non_sleep_alternative`): narrated idea explainers, calm audio for dogs
left home alone, slow-TV scenery wallpaper, and short guided meditation.

**They exist to price the opportunity cost of the whole portfolio.** The
narrated explainer carries what the concept file calls the single most valuable
unknown: the actual RPM differential between explainer and ambient categories.
If that gap is large, it redirects everything — which is why the explainer
scores high on generalizability despite being unlike everything else here.

The explainer also raises the quality bar in a way the ambient concepts never
do: [[Local Narration Capability]] is comfortably adequate for a sleeping
listener and is an open question for an attentive one.

Slow-TV scenery is the concept most directly exposed to
[[YouTube Inauthentic Content Policy]] — its open question is precisely whether
still-based slow TV is accepted at all, or whether real motion footage is
required. Guided meditation adds a wellness-policy constraint and needs TTS
pause and pacing control the current voice has not been tested for.
