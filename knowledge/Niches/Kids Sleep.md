# Kids Sleep

Lullabies, infant white noise, music-box arrangements and nature-lullaby
hybrids — four concepts under `niche: kids_sleep`, typically 1-2h.

**The decisive unknown is not creative, it is economic.** Made-for-Kids
treatment changes ad revenue, and the realised RPM under that treatment is
recorded as the number that decides the whole category. Until it is known,
production time spent here is speculative. Two further open items — current
COPPA obligations for a channel of this shape, and whether music-generation
output can be cleanly licensed for commercial use — are tracked in
[[Open Research Questions]].

**Licensing is the structural blocker.** Every melodic concept here needs a
music route that survives [[Rights and Licensing]]. Public-domain lullabies are
the safest path; generated music is the fastest path and the least certain one.

The infant white-noise concept is the exception: it is a near-clone of the
production shape already proven in [[Adult Sleep]], and inherits the same
synthesis-only dependency profile.
